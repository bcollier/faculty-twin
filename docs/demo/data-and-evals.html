What goes into the twin, and how we test it

An AI version of Prof. Ben Collier that answers course questions from his own slides and what he said in class, in an AI voice made from his recordings. All counts are aggregates. No student names or course text appear on this page.

How a class becomes searchable

CollectSlide PDFs, PowerPoint notes, Zoom recordings and captions, notebooks, from Google Drive and Zoom.
Read each slidePDF text, speaker notes, and text recognized inside images (278 picture slides).
Protect studentsEvery name except mine replaced, student turns removed, cursing smoothed, quiz codes removed.
Match video to slidesA frame every 2 seconds is matched to the slide on screen, so each slide gets the minutes I spent on it.
Cut clipsOnly stretches where I alone am speaking, with nobody named and no student presentations.
Embed and upload1,143 records embedded with Voyage AI, checked against the rosters, stored in a private Supabase bucket.

Where a student's answer comes from

  • My FAQ for logistics like office hours and missed classes, with a booking link or the TA's contact.
  • My slides for course concepts, narrated from the slide, my notes, and my class explanation, with the course, session, date and slide number.
  • A class clip of me explaining that slide, where one passed every privacy rule.
  • A clean decline when nothing scores at least 0.52, my hand-chosen threshold.

Everything sits behind a course passcode. The app runs on Vercel, content and logs on Supabase.

Part 2

How we test it: the eval harness

22 real questions students emailed me, de-identified, run through the twin and judged by two AI models from different companies, so no model only grades its own work.

Real questions22 student emails, names and details removed, each with my actual reply as the reference.
Calibrate the judges8 made-up cases with known right answers. Both judges got 8 of 8 before we trusted them.
Ask the twinThe real index, my retrieval code and the 0.52 threshold, exactly as students use it.
Two judges scoreClaude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol each give six scores from 1 to 5 and a pass or fail.
CompareAgainst a generic chatbot with no course material, answering the same 22 questions.

What the twin does better

  • Picks the right call, answer or decline: 77% of questions.
  • Declines logistics it should not handle: meeting requests passed 83%; grading, career, Canvas access and team questions 100%.
  • Stays in scope and PG: scope 4.05 vs 3.14, safety and tone 4.62 vs 3.80.

What the judges flagged, and what changed

  • Read a quiz access code from a transcript. Fixed codes removed from the index.
  • Narrated slides for logistics questions. Fixed logistics routing and my FAQ now answer them.
  • Some answers mix slides from both courses. Open next change to segment selection.
  • Narration sometimes adds specifics beyond the slide. Open tighter grounding check.

The Oct 7 run predates both fixes. Next run measures them.
