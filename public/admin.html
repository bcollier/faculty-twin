      Voice
      
        
      Three kinds of voice. My clone and the other ElevenLabs voices cost ElevenLabs credits per character; the Microsoft voices are free. Students see a label that matches the voice: "AI voice made from my recordings." only for my clone, "AI voice (a stock voice, not mine)." for any other voice, and no voice label for captions only. Changing the voice means each answer's audio is made fresh the next time it plays.

        Limits and access
        
        
        
          
        
            
        
              
              
              
        Characters sent to ElevenLabs per day (this costs money). Past the cap, answers continue with captions only, or with the free fallback voice if it is on. Zero turns ElevenLabs off.
        
            
        
            
        
              
              
              
        Characters spoken by the free Microsoft voices per day. They cost nothing, but the service is free, so keep a ceiling. One visitor can use at most a quarter of either cap.
        
            
        
            
            
        
            
        Per-visitor limits
        
            
        
          
        
          
        
            
        
              
              
            
        
            
        
              
              
            
        
            
        At least 6 characters. The server stores only a hash. Students already signed in keep access until their session cookie runs out.
